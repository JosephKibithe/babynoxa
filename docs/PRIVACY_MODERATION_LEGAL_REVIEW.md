# Privacy, moderation, and legal review packet

**Status: qualified review pending. This document is not legal advice.**

## Data inventory

The service processes public wallet addresses, token/launch events, metadata URLs,
project website/social links, comment text, moderation reasons, transaction hashes,
IP/network logs at the hosting layer, and administrator identifiers. No design need
exists for legal names, government identifiers, precise location, contacts, seed
phrases, or private keys.

## Decisions requiring qualified adults/professionals

- Supported jurisdictions, sanctions/geofencing obligations, and prohibited users.
- Whether token creation/trading, fee sharing, and treasury activity trigger financial,
  securities, money-transmission, consumer-protection, gambling, tax, or disclosure rules.
- Minimum age, guardian/consent model, and treatment of minors.
- Controller/processor roles, legal bases, retention periods, deletion/access rights,
  cross-border transfers, subprocessors, cookies/analytics, and breach notices.
- Terms of service, privacy notice, risk disclosures, acceptable-use policy, IP/content
  takedowns, appeals, law-enforcement requests, and emergency communications.
- GPL obligations for the modified V2-derived distribution.

## Moderation baseline for review

Moderation affects only offchain comments. Hide/restore actions require authorization,
reason, actor, timestamp, and immutable audit entry. Moderators cannot alter onchain
metadata or economic state. A production policy must define prohibited content,
reports, appeals, response times, moderator access review, retention, transparency,
and escalation for credible threats or illegal material.

The qualified reviewer must identify themselves, jurisdiction/scope, date, required
changes, accepted risks, and sign-off. Until then this Phase 14 gate remains open.
